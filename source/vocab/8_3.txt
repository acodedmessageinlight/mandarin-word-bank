#8.3|城市的著名景点|Places of Interest
神秘|shén mì|mysterious|阿拉伯是一个神秘的地方。|Arabia is a mysterious place.
地方|dì fang|place|这个地方有很多美丽的风景。|This place has lots of beautiful scenery.
从小|cóng xiǎo|since young|妹妹从小就喜欢画画。|My sister has loved drawing since she was little.
帆船|fān chuán|sailing boat|我们城市每年都有帆船比赛。|Our city has a sailing race every year.
却|què|but; however|我参加了比赛，却没有获奖。|I took part in the competition but didn't win a prize.
外观|wài guān|external appearance|这辆自行车的外观漂亮，我非常喜欢。|This bike looks great; I really like it.
想像|xiǎng xiàng|imagine|我想像中的阿拉伯国家有沙漠、有骆驼。|The Arab countries I imagine have deserts and camels.
壮观|zhuàng guān|magnificent|长城比我想像中的还要壮观。|The Great Wall is even more magnificent than I imagined.
吃惊|chī jīng|surprised|看到她瘦了那么多，我们都很吃惊。|We were all surprised to see she'd lost so much weight.
不管|bù guǎn|no matter|不管你喜欢不喜欢，你都要学数学。|Whether you like it or not, you have to study maths.
长袍|cháng páo|robe|阿拉伯女人大部分时间都穿着长袍。|Arab women wear robes most of the time.
戴|dài|put on or wear (accessories)|他戴着一只蓝色的手表。|He is wearing a blue watch.
面纱|miàn shā|veil|她戴着面纱，我看不到她的样子。|She's wearing a veil; I can't see what she looks like.
外面|wài miàn|outside|外面正在下雨，我们还是等一下再出门。|It's raining outside; let's wait a bit before going out.
任何|rèn hé|any|妈妈说任何有营养的食物都要吃。|Mum says we should eat any nutritious food.
地位|dì wèi|status|在一些国家，妇女的地位很低。|In some countries, women have low status.
*阿拉伯|Ā lā bó|Arab; Arabic|他会说阿拉伯语。|He can speak Arabic.
*迪拜|Dí bài|Dubai|迪拜有七星级的帆船酒店。|Dubai has the "seven-star" Burj Al Arab hotel.
景点|jǐng diǎn|tourist attraction|这么多好玩的景点，先去哪里呢？|So many fun attractions; where shall we go first?
标志性|biāo zhì xìng|symbolic|我想看这个城市最有标志性的建筑。|I want to see this city's most iconic building.
遗产|yí chǎn|heritage|很多城市的景点都计划申请成为世界文化遗产。|Many cities plan to apply for World Heritage status for their attractions.
建设|jiàn shè|build|这个城市建设得真快！|This city is being built up so fast!
正式|zhèng shì|official|昨天我正式上班了，在一个咖啡馆当服务员。|Yesterday I officially started work as a waiter in a café.
落成|luò chéng|(building, bridge, etc.) be completed|新落成的大桥离我家很近。|The newly completed bridge is very close to my home.
设计师|shè jì shī|designer|哥哥长大以后想当一个服装设计师。|My brother wants to be a fashion designer when he grows up.
游览|yóu lǎn|tour|我们昨天游览了悉尼歌剧院。|We toured the Sydney Opera House yesterday.
项目|xiàng mù|project|这个项目很重要，一定要做好。|This project is important; it must be done well.
引人之处|yǐn rén zhī chù|attraction|这首歌的引人之处是歌词写得特别感人。|What's appealing about this song is its especially moving lyrics.
贝壳|bèi ké|seashell|昨天我在海边捡到了一个红色的贝壳。|Yesterday I found a red seashell at the beach.
空中的|kōng zhōng de|in the air|我想象空中的鸟儿一样，自由自在的。|I want to be free like a bird in the sky.
花瓣|huā bàn|petal|秋天到了，地上有很多花瓣，好美！|Autumn is here and there are lots of petals on the ground; so pretty!
错过|cuò guò|miss; let slip|这是一个很好的机会，你千万不要错过。|This is a great opportunity; don't miss it.
到达|dào dá|reach; arrive|从这里坐巴士就能到达市中心。|From here you can reach the city centre by bus.
乘坐|chéng zuò|ride; travel by|爸爸每天乘坐公司的巴士上班。|Dad takes the company bus to work every day.
市区|shì qū|city area|市区里有很多高楼大厦。|There are many tall buildings in the city area.
穿梭巴士|chuān suō bā shì|shuttle bus|我要乘坐机场免费穿梭巴士去酒店。|I'll take the free airport shuttle bus to the hotel.
下车|xià chē|alight|我们在下一站下车。|We get off at the next stop.
即可|jí kě|able to|只要上网报名即可成为会员。|Just register online to become a member.
开放|kāi fàng|open (to the public)|这家博物馆每天的开放时间很短，只有四个小时。|This museum is only open four hours a day.
门票|mén piào|admission ticket|这个公园的门票不贵，才五块钱一张。|This park's tickets aren't expensive, only five yuan each.
*悉尼|Xī ní|Sydney|悉尼是澳大利亚第一大城市。|Sydney is Australia's largest city.
*悉尼歌剧院|Xī ní Gē jù yuàn|Sydney Opera House|悉尼歌剧院是世界文化遗产之一。|The Sydney Opera House is a World Heritage site.
*情人港|Qíng rén Gǎng|Darling Harbour|晚上的情人港很漂亮。|Darling Harbour is beautiful at night.
*海湾大桥|Hǎi wān Dà qiáo|Harbour Bridge|我们走路过了海湾大桥。|We walked across the Harbour Bridge.
*维多利亚女王大厦|Wéi duō lì yà nǚ wáng Dà shà|Queen Victoria Building|维多利亚女王大厦里有很多商店。|The Queen Victoria Building has lots of shops.
*丹麦|Dān mài|Denmark|悉尼歌剧院的设计师是丹麦人。|The Sydney Opera House's designer was Danish.
