#1.3|我的宠物|My Pet
宠物|chǒng wù|pet|你家有什么宠物？|What pets does your family have?
仓鼠|cāng shǔ|hamster|我家的仓鼠很大。|Our hamster is big.
兔子|tù zi|rabbit|我家有三只兔子。|We have three rabbits.
小鸟|xiǎo niǎo|bird|小鸟爱唱歌。|Birds love singing.
蛇|shé|snake|我不喜欢蛇。弟弟喜欢蛇。|I don't like snakes. My brother likes snakes.
都|dōu|both; all|我们都喜欢小狗。|We all like puppies.
养|yǎng|raise; keep|你养宠物吗？|Do you keep pets?
有时候|yǒu shí hou|sometimes|有时候我喜欢它，有时候我不喜欢。|Sometimes I like it, sometimes I don't.
觉得|jué de|feel; think|我觉得小猫很可爱。|I think kittens are very cute.
每天|měi tiān|every day|我每天都学中文。|I study Chinese every day.
和……一起|hé…yì qǐ|with|他喜欢和我一起玩。|He likes playing with me.
玩|wán|play|你和宠物一起玩什么？|What do you play with your pet?
不太|bú tài|not very|我不太喜欢仓鼠。|I don't really like hamsters.
知道|zhī dào|know|你知道小狗在哪里吗？|Do you know where the puppy is?
小朋友|xiǎo péng yǒu|young children|小朋友喜欢小动物。|Young children like little animals.
乌龟|wū guī|tortoise|乌龟是个很可爱的动物。|A tortoise is a very cute animal.
安静|ān jìng|quiet|我家旁边是一个学校，很不安静。|There's a school next to my home; it's not quiet at all.
吓了一跳|xià le yí tiào|become startled|小狗把我吓了一跳。|The puppy gave me a fright.
房间|fáng jiān|room|我家有三个房间。|My home has three rooms.
门口|mén kǒu|doorway|我家门口有一只小狗。|There's a puppy at my door.
再也不|zài yě bù|will not ever|我再也不养鸟了。|I'll never keep birds again.
*站|zhàn|stand|小鸟站在桌子上。|The bird is standing on the table.
*滑倒|huá dǎo|slip and fall|玛丽在门口滑倒了。|Mary slipped and fell at the door.
*哭|kū|cry|妹妹哭了。|My little sister cried.
*踩|cǎi|step on|她踩到了乌龟。|She stepped on the tortoise.
*跑|pǎo|run|小猫跑了出来。|The kitten ran out.
*桌子|zhuō zi|table|桌子上有一只小鸟。|There's a bird on the table.
*箱子|xiāng zi|box; case|桌子旁边有一个大箱子。|There's a big box next to the table.
*可是|kě shì|but; however|我喜欢小狗，可是妈妈不喜欢。|I like puppies, but Mum doesn't.
*白色|bái sè|white|我的小狗是白色的。|My puppy is white.
*黑色|hēi sè|black|我家的猫是黑色的。|Our cat is black.
*红色|hóng sè|red|我喜欢红色。|I like red.
*黄色|huáng sè|yellow|小鸟是黄色的。|The bird is yellow.
*蓝色|lán sè|blue|天空是蓝色的。|The sky is blue.
*绿色|lǜ sè|green|乌龟是绿色的。|The tortoise is green.
*橙色|chéng sè|orange|我的书包是橙色的。|My school bag is orange.
*紫色|zǐ sè|purple|姐姐喜欢紫色。|My older sister likes purple.
*棕色|zōng sè|brown|兔子是棕色的。|The rabbit is brown.
*灰色|huī sè|grey|仓鼠是灰色的。|The hamster is grey.
