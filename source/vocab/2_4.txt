#2.4|学校生活|Life in School
书包|shū bāo|school bag|我的书包很大很漂亮。|My school bag is big and pretty.
重|zhòng|heavy|我的书包很重。|My school bag is heavy.
帮|bāng|help|他帮我拿电脑。|He helps me carry my computer.
拿|ná|take|今天我去拿我的电脑。|Today I'm going to collect my computer.
课本|kè běn|textbook|我有很多课本。|I have lots of textbooks.
笔记本|bǐ jì běn|notebook|我的中文笔记本很重。|My Chinese notebook is heavy.
字典|zì diǎn|dictionary|我们教室有很多中文字典。|Our classroom has lots of Chinese dictionaries.
文具|wén jù|stationery|我们每天用很多文具。|We use lots of stationery every day.
那么|nà me|so|他的课外活动那么多！|He has so many extracurricular activities!
节|jié|(used for class periods)|我上午有四节课。|I have four lessons in the morning.
种|zhǒng|type|我的书包里有八种课本。|There are eight kinds of textbooks in my bag.
浴巾|yù jīn|towel|我今天没有带浴巾。|I didn't bring a towel today.
可怜|kě lián|pitiful|他很可怜，每天的作业很多很多。|Poor him, he has loads of homework every day.
电梯|diàn tī|lift|电梯坏了，不可以坐。|The lift is broken; you can't use it.
借书|jiè shū|borrow book|我常常去图书馆借书。|I often borrow books from the library.
找|zhǎo|find|我找到一本喜欢的书。|I found a book I like.
很久|hěn jiǔ|a long time|我很久才把这本书读完。|It took me a long time to finish this book.
最后|zuì hòu|last|他考了最后一名。|He came last in the exam.
兴奋|xīng fèn|excited|他今天很兴奋，因为他买了新电脑。|He's very excited today because he bought a new computer.
生气|shēng qì|angry|我考得不好，妈妈很生气。|I did badly in the exam and Mum was angry.
图书管理员|tú shū guǎn lǐ yuán|librarian|我们的图书管理员很好。|Our librarian is very nice.
小说|xiǎo shuō|novel|我不喜欢看小说。|I don't like reading novels.
杂志|zá zhì|magazine|这个图书馆里没有杂志。|This library has no magazines.
还|huán|return|明天要记得还书。|Remember to return the books tomorrow.|环
丢|diū|lose; misplace|我的书包丢了。|I've lost my school bag.
想来想去|xiǎng lái xiǎng qù|make considerations|我想来想去，还是没参加比赛。|After thinking it over, I still didn't enter the competition.
想不起来|xiǎng bu qǐ lái|cannot recall|我想不起来有没有读过这本书。|I can't remember whether I've read this book.
办法|bàn fǎ|solution; way; means|我没有办法。|There's nothing I can do.
弄错|nòng cuò|make a mistake|老师说他弄错了我的名字。|The teacher said he got my name wrong.
马上|mǎ shàng|immediately|我一回到家，马上就去做作业。|As soon as I get home, I do my homework straight away.
哭笑不得|kū xiào bù dé|not knowing whether to cry or to laugh|好朋友生气了，我哭笑不得。|My best friend got angry, and I didn't know whether to laugh or cry.
小卖部|xiǎo mài bù|minimart|我们学校有两个小卖部。|Our school has two tuck shops.
最爱|zuì ài|favourite|小妹妹的最爱是冰淇淋。|My little sister's favourite is ice cream.
除了…以外…|chú le…yǐ wài…|in addition to|除了课本以外，书包里还有文具。|Besides textbooks, there's also stationery in the bag.
练习本|liàn xí běn|exercise book|我的练习本丢了。|I've lost my exercise book.
原因|yuán yīn|reason|我没去小卖部的原因是因为没有钱。|The reason I didn't go to the tuck shop is that I had no money.
真正的|zhēn zhèng de|real|我真正的爱好是唱歌。|My real hobby is singing.
各种各样|gè zhǒng gè yàng|all kinds of|小卖部卖各种各样的文具。|The tuck shop sells all kinds of stationery.
糖果|táng guǒ|sweets|我们爱吃糖果。|We love eating sweets.
点心|diǎn xīn|snacks|餐厅的点心很好吃。|The canteen's snacks are delicious.
冰淇淋|bīng qí lín|ice cream|我一次可以吃三个冰淇淋。|I can eat three ice creams at once.
喝|hē|drink|每天要多喝水。|Drink plenty of water every day.
可乐|kě lè|cola|不要经常喝可乐。|Don't drink cola too often.
健康|jiàn kāng|healthy|我们要吃健康的中餐。|We should eat healthy lunches.
可惜|kě xī|pity|可惜我们学校的小卖部不卖糖果。|It's a pity our tuck shop doesn't sell sweets.
排队|pái duì|queue up|很多同学排队买冰淇淋。|Lots of students queue up to buy ice cream.
