#6.3|健康的生活方式|Healthy Lifestyle
压力|yā lì|pressure|快要考试了，大家的压力都很大。|Exams are coming; everyone is under a lot of pressure.
生活|shēng huó|life|读高中了，我的生活有很大变化。|Since starting senior high, my life has changed a lot.
大吃一惊|dà chī yì jīng|shocked; surprised|看到这个手机的价格，我大吃一惊。|I was shocked when I saw the price of this phone.
各自的|gè zì de|each; respective|我们是好朋友，但都有各自的爱好。|We're good friends, but each has our own hobbies.
方式|fāng shì|way; pattern|我们要有健康的生活方式。|We should have a healthy lifestyle.
做功课|zuò gōng kè|do homework|我和好朋友每天在一起做功课。|My best friend and I do homework together every day.
半夜|bàn yè|midnight|朋友的生日晚会到半夜才结束。|My friend's birthday party didn't end until midnight.
果汁|guǒ zhī|fruit juice|果汁是健康饮料。|Fruit juice is a healthy drink.
赶|gǎn|try to catch; rush for|早上我起床晚了，赶巴士赶得很辛苦。|I got up late this morning and had a hard time catching the bus.
上网|shàng wǎng|go online|我经常上网听音乐。|I often go online to listen to music.
超过|chāo guò|exceed|我的Instagram的粉丝人数超过了一百，我真开心！|My Instagram followers have passed one hundred; I'm so happy!
容易|róng yì|easy; simple|这次考试很容易，我只花了半个小时就做完了。|This exam was easy; I finished it in just half an hour.
近视|jìn shì|myopia; short-sightedness|要保护眼睛，防止近视。|Protect your eyes to prevent short-sightedness.
甚至|shèn zhì|even to the extent that|他甚至不知道父母的生日。|He doesn't even know his parents' birthdays.
抽烟|chōu yān|smoking|抽烟对身体有害。|Smoking is harmful to your health.
心情|xīn qíng|mood|要放假了，大家的心情都很好。|The holidays are coming, and everyone is in a good mood.
糟糕|zāo gāo|bad; terrible|今天的天气真糟糕。|The weather today is terrible.
没意思|méi yì si|meaningless|上网太多没意思。|Spending too much time online is pointless.
节奏|jié zòu|rhythm|我们的生活节奏越来越快，真累啊！|The pace of our lives is getting faster and faster; so tiring!
紧张|jǐn zhāng|nervous|考试前我非常紧张，考完后就不紧张了。|I was very nervous before the exam, but not after it.
舒服|shū fu|comfortable|他舒服地躺在沙发上看报纸。|He lay comfortably on the sofa reading the newspaper.
于是|yú shì|so; therefore|哥哥最近经常生病，于是他开始每天做运动。|My brother has been ill a lot lately, so he started exercising every day.
要求|yāo qiú|expectation; request|老师对我们的要求很高。|The teacher has high expectations of us.
注重|zhù zhòng|pay attention to|这个国家不太注重饮食健康，所以胖子很多。|This country doesn't pay much attention to healthy eating, so many people are overweight.
逼|bī|force|妈妈每天逼弟弟吃蔬菜。|Mum forces my little brother to eat vegetables every day.
戒烟|jiè yān|quit smoking|爸爸为了身体健康，昨天又开始戒烟了。|For his health, Dad started quitting smoking again yesterday.
戒酒|jiè jiǔ|quit drinking|戒烟戒酒并不是一件容易的事情。|Quitting smoking and drinking is not easy.
动力|dòng lì|motivation|学习上的压力也会变成动力。|Study pressure can also become motivation.
歇|xiē|rest|走累了，我们就坐下来歇一下。|When we're tired of walking, let's sit down and rest.
遭罪|zāo zuì|endure tough conditions|出门遇上了刮大风、下大雨，真遭罪。|We ran into strong wind and heavy rain when we went out; what an ordeal.
控制|kòng zhì|control|我最近又长胖了，看来要控制饮食了。|I've put on weight again; looks like I need to control my diet.
网瘾|wǎng yǐn|internet addiction|我哥哥网瘾很大，没有网络他觉得没意思。|My brother is very addicted to the internet; without it he feels bored.
叹气|tàn qì|sigh|他考试考得不好，一直在叹气。|He did badly in the exam and keeps sighing.
有利于|yǒu lì yú|be beneficial to|新鲜空气有利于健康。|Fresh air is good for your health.
加班|jiā bān|work overtime|爸爸整天没完没了地加班，我已经三天没有见到他了。|Dad works overtime endlessly; I haven't seen him for three days.
赚钱|zhuàn qián|make money|你觉得赚钱很重要吗？我不这么觉得。|Do you think making money is important? I don't.
