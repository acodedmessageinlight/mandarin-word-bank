#4.3|爱好|Hobbies
爱好|ài hào|hobby|她的爱好很多。|She has lots of hobbies.
中餐|zhōng cān|Chinese cuisine|我觉得中餐很好吃。|I think Chinese food is delicious.
猜|cāi|guess|请猜猜看这是谁写的中文。|Guess who wrote this Chinese.
滑冰|huá bīng|ice-skating|我滑冰滑得很好。|I skate very well.
怕|pà|fear; afraid of|我怕输掉比赛。|I'm afraid of losing the match.
摔跤|shuāi jiāo|have a fall|我弟弟经常摔跤，他两岁。|My little brother often falls over; he's two.
滑雪|huá xuě|skiing|我喜欢滑雪。|I like skiing.
下雪|xià xuě|snowing|今年怎么没有下雪？|Why hasn't it snowed this year?
滑板|huá bǎn|skateboard|每天下午我都和妹妹一起去玩滑板。|Every afternoon my sister and I go skateboarding.
滑水|huá shuǐ|water skiing|在澳大利亚很多人滑水。|Many people water-ski in Australia.
笑|xiào|smile; laugh|老师说要多笑笑。|The teacher says we should smile more.
滑滑梯|huá huá tī|go down the slide|我觉得滑滑梯很好玩。|I think going down the slide is fun.
高尔夫球|gāo ěr fū qiú|golf|爸爸每周打两次高尔夫球。|Dad plays golf twice a week.
捡球|jiǎn qiú|retrieve balls|打完球要记得捡球。|Remember to pick up the balls after playing.
无聊|wú liáo|bored|有爱好就不会无聊。|If you have hobbies you won't be bored.
球场|qiú chǎng|ball court|今天球场上有篮球比赛。|There's a basketball game on the court today.
加油|jiā yóu|cheer (someone) on|我去给足球比赛的同学们加油。|I'm going to cheer on my classmates at the football match.
工作人员|gōng zuò rén yuán|staff on duty|这里的工作人员很有礼貌。|The staff here are very polite.
打招呼|dǎ zhāo hu|greet|我每天都跟邻居打招呼。|I greet my neighbours every day.
明白|míng bai|understand|妈妈的话，你听明白了吗？|Did you understand what Mum said?
怀疑|huái yí|suspect|我怀疑我的英文老师会说中文。|I suspect my English teacher can speak Chinese.
做生意|zuò shēng yi|run a business|爸爸的朋友们都在做生意。|Dad's friends all run businesses.
抽时间|chōu shí jiān|make time|老师说每天都要抽时间学习。|The teacher says we should make time to study every day.
语言|yǔ yán|language|学语言要多听多说。|To learn a language, listen and speak a lot.
与众不同|yǔ zhòng bù tóng|special|他的爱好与众不同。|His hobby is unusual.
运动|yùn dòng|exercise|天天运动身体好。|Exercising every day is good for you.
经济|jīng jì|economics|以后我想学经济。|In future I want to study economics.
迷上|mí shàng|addicted to|我们不能迷上网络游戏。|We mustn't get hooked on online games.
脱口秀|tuō kǒu xiù|talk show|现在的脱口秀节目越来越多。|There are more and more talk shows now.
留|liú|save|我留了一个苹果给妹妹。|I saved an apple for my little sister.
字条|zì tiáo|note|桌子上有一张字条。|There's a note on the table.
亲爱的|qīn ài de|dearly beloved|我最亲爱的人是爸爸妈妈。|The people dearest to me are Mum and Dad.
信心|xìn xīn|confidence|我总是对自己很有信心。|I'm always confident in myself.
西装|xī zhuāng|suit|哥哥穿上西装很帅。|My brother looks handsome in a suit.
油|yóu|fuel; petrol|我们去给汽车加油。|We're going to fill the car with petrol.
