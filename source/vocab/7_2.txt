#7.2|度假|Going on Holiday
假期|jià qī|holiday; vacation|这个假期，我想好好在家休息。|This holiday I want to have a good rest at home.
春天|chūn tiān|spring|春天到了，公园里开满了美丽的花儿。|Spring is here and the park is full of beautiful flowers.
复活节|Fù huó jié|Easter|人们喜欢在复活节的时候吃彩蛋。|People like eating Easter eggs at Easter.
另外|lìng wài|in addition; besides|她买了一条裙子，另外还买了一双鞋。|She bought a skirt, and a pair of shoes as well.
大部分|dà bù fèn|majority; most|大部分人都会用手机上网。|Most people can go online with their phones.
度假|dù jià|go on holiday|他们下星期到海边度假。|They're going on holiday to the seaside next week.
签证|qiān zhèng|visa|有些国家需要办签证才能到那里旅游。|Some countries require a visa before you can travel there.
机票|jī piào|air ticket|现在去马来西亚的机票好便宜，我们赶快上网订吧。|Flights to Malaysia are really cheap now; let's book online quickly.
入关|rù guān|entering a country|今天入关的人特别多，排了一个小时的队还没有轮到我。|So many people were going through immigration today; I queued an hour and it still wasn't my turn.
取|qǔ|take; get; fetch|我先去取行李，你在这里等我。|I'll go get the luggage first; wait for me here.
顺利|shùn lì|smooth|工作进行得很顺利，按时完成没问题。|Work is going smoothly; finishing on time is no problem.
成排的|chéng pái de|in rows|那棵树上站着成排的鸟儿，真有意思。|Rows of birds are standing on that tree; how interesting.
摩托车|mó tuō chē|motorcycle|在台湾，很多人骑摩托车。|In Taiwan, many people ride motorcycles.
从来|cóng lái|all along|我从来没见过他。|I have never seen him.
狮城|Shī Chéng|Lion City (Singapore)|狮城，也就是新加坡，是东南亚的一个小国。|The Lion City, i.e. Singapore, is a small country in Southeast Asia.
世界|shì jiè|world|世界很大，我想去看看。|The world is big; I want to go and see it.
吸引力|xī yǐn lì|attraction|泰国的吸引力太大了，每年有很多游客。|Thailand is so attractive; it has many tourists every year.
一年到头|yì nián dào tóu|all year round|我一年到头都很忙。|I'm busy all year round.
夏天|xià tiān|summer|这个夏天特别热。|This summer is especially hot.
适合|shì hé|suitable|这里的生活很适合老人。|Life here is very suitable for elderly people.
孩子|hái zi|children|王老师有两个可爱的孩子。|Teacher Wang has two lovely children.
动物园|dòng wù yuán|zoo|今天我们一家人到动物园去玩。|Today our family went to the zoo.
熊猫|xióng māo|panda|妹妹非常喜欢熊猫，因为熊猫长得很可爱。|My sister loves pandas because they look so cute.
实在|shí zài|really; indeed|我最近实在太忙了，每天只睡五六个小时。|I've been really busy lately, sleeping only five or six hours a day.
飞鸟|fēi niǎo|birds|这里的飞鸟很多，常常可以听到它们的叫声。|There are many birds here; you can often hear them calling.
值得|zhí dé|worth|马尔代夫很值得去玩一趟。|The Maldives is well worth a trip.
购物中心|gòu wù zhōng xīn|shopping centre|这里的购物中心很大，东西很多。|The shopping centre here is big with lots of things.
天堂|tiān táng|paradise|新加坡是个美食天堂，到处都可以吃到好吃的东西。|Singapore is a food paradise; you can find tasty food everywhere.
除非|chú fēi|unless|今个周末我不能出去玩，除非今天就把所有的作业做完。|I can't go out this weekend unless I finish all my homework today.
大减价|dà jiǎn jià|sale|圣诞节大减价的时候，购物中心总是人山人海。|During the Christmas sales, shopping centres are always packed.
巴刹|bā shā|market|很多新加坡人都喜欢到巴刹，也就是市场，去买东西。|Many Singaporeans like shopping at the pasar, i.e. the market.|巴沙
讨价还价|tǎo jià huán jià|bargain|在市场买东西，一定要讨价还价。|When shopping at a market, you must bargain.
