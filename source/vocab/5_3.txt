#5.3|饮食文化|Food and Culture
菜谱|cài pǔ|recipe|我觉得看中餐菜谱很难。|I find Chinese recipes hard to read.
奇怪|qí guài|weird; strange|最近天气很奇怪。|The weather has been strange lately.
少量|shǎo liàng|low in quantity|菜里放少量的盐就行了。|Just add a little salt to the dish.
适量|shì liàng|appropriate amount|吃什么都要适量。|Eat everything in moderation.
不一样|bù yí yàng|not the same; different|我和弟弟的口味很不一样。|My brother and I have very different tastes.
好玩|hǎo wán|fun|做菜很花时间，但也很好玩。|Cooking takes time, but it's fun.
试一试|shì yí shì|give a try|我想什么菜都要试一试。|I want to try every dish.
室友|shì yǒu|roommate|我的室友性格很外向。|My roommate is very outgoing.
比萨饼|bǐ sà bǐng|pizza|这家餐馆的比萨饼非常有名。|This restaurant's pizza is very famous.
够|gòu|enough|米饭如果不够，可以再加。|If there isn't enough rice, you can have more.
蔬菜|shū cài|vegetable|每天要多吃蔬菜少吃肉。|Eat more vegetables and less meat every day.
沙拉|shā lā|salad|沙拉健康又可口。|Salad is healthy and tasty.
买单|mǎi dān|pay the bill|今天谁买单？|Who's paying today?
麻烦|má fan|troublesome|坐巴士去太麻烦了。|Going by bus is too much hassle.
AA制|AA zhì|go Dutch|我和朋友吃饭经常都是AA制。|My friends and I usually split the bill.|诶诶制
同意|tóng yì|agree|同意这个建议的人请举手。|Raise your hand if you agree with this suggestion.
甜点|tián diǎn|dessert|她觉得西式甜点很好吃。|She thinks Western desserts are delicious.
轮流|lún liú|take turns|大家要轮流来做饭。|Everyone should take turns cooking.
付|fù|pay|我们先吃完再付钱。|We'll eat first and pay afterwards.
高兴|gāo xìng|happy|又见到老朋友，我特别高兴。|I'm really happy to see my old friend again.
特地|tè dì|specially|我特地去菜市场买了新鲜的蔬菜。|I went to the market specially to buy fresh vegetables.
查看|chá kàn|check|他经常用手机查看他的电邮。|He often checks his email on his phone.
注意|zhù yì|mind; pay attention to|过马路要注意交通。|Watch the traffic when crossing the road.
水果|shuǐ guǒ|fruit|这个菜市场的蔬菜和水果特别新鲜。|This market's vegetables and fruit are very fresh.
惊讶|jīng yà|surprised|他的游泳水平让人惊讶。|His swimming level is surprising.
饭菜|fàn cài|dishes|餐厅的饭菜很可口。|The canteen's food is tasty.
筷子|kuài zi|chopsticks|中国人用筷子吃饭。|Chinese people eat with chopsticks.
钓|diào|fish with a hook and line|周末我经常和爸爸出去钓鱼。|At weekends I often go fishing with Dad.
水池|shuǐ chí|pond|水池里除了有鱼，还有乌龟。|Besides fish, there are tortoises in the pond.
抓|zhuā|catch|我抓到了好几只虾。|I caught several prawns.
盘子|pán zi|plate|他把食物放在盘子上。|He put the food on the plate.
糖醋鱼|táng cù yú|sweet and sour fish|你想不想尝尝糖醋鱼？|Do you want to try sweet and sour fish?
礼貌|lǐ mào|polite|学生对老师要有礼貌。|Students should be polite to teachers.
吃饱|chī bǎo|full (from eating)|吃饱了，我们就出去走走。|Once we're full, let's go for a walk.
剩|shèng|leftover; surplus|我买完单，只剩下三块钱了。|After paying the bill I only had three yuan left.
万能|wàn néng|all powerful|钱不是万能的。|Money can't do everything.
*意大利|Yì dà lì|Italy|我们去了一家意大利饭馆。|We went to an Italian restaurant.
