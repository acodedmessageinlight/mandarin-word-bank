#3.4|我家周围的环境|My Neighbourhood
草|cǎo|grass|公园里的花草真漂亮。|The flowers and grass in the park are beautiful.
树|shù|tree|我们学校有很多树。|Our school has lots of trees.
湖|hú|lake|这个湖真美。|This lake is really beautiful.
篮球场|lán qiú chǎng|basketball court|我经常去篮球场打篮球。|I often go to the court to play basketball.
也许|yě xǔ|maybe; perhaps|也许明天会下雨。|Maybe it will rain tomorrow.
周围|zhōu wéi|surrounding|我家周围有很多高楼。|There are lots of tall buildings around my home.
环境|huán jìng|environment|这个公寓楼的环境很好。|This apartment block has a nice environment.
齐全|qí quán|complete|学校的设施很齐全。|The school's facilities are complete.
车站|chē zhàn|bus stop|我早上到车站去等校车。|In the morning I wait for the school bus at the stop.
旁边|páng biān|beside; next to|我家旁边有一个电影院。|There's a cinema next to my home.
便利店|biàn lì diàn|convenience store|我家离便利店很近。|My home is close to a convenience store.
对面|duì miàn|opposite|学校的对面有一个公园。|There's a park opposite the school.
青少年|qīng shào nián|adolescent; teenager|青少年应该多运动。|Teenagers should exercise more.
活动中心|huó dòng zhōng xīn|activity centre|爷爷去老年活动中心打球。|Grandpa plays ball at the senior activity centre.
后面|hòu miàn|back; behind|公园的后面有一个游乐场。|Behind the park is a playground.
喂|wèi|feed|我每天喂小狗吃饭。|I feed my puppy every day.
鸭子|yā zi|duck|鸭子在湖里游来游去。|The ducks swim around on the lake.
事情|shì qing|matter; task|我每天都有很多事情要做。|I have lots of things to do every day.
最近|zuì jìn|recently|他最近要参加游泳比赛。|He's taking part in a swimming competition soon.
专心|zhuān xīn|focused|我上课很专心。|I concentrate hard in class.
醒|xǐng|awake|他早上六点就醒了。|He woke up at six in the morning.
菜市场|cài shì chǎng|market|妈妈每天去菜市场买菜。|Mum goes to the market every day to buy food.
小贩|xiǎo fàn|hawker|小贩们很早就出门工作。|The hawkers go out to work very early.
其中|qí zhōng|among (which, them, etc.)|我家有三只宠物，其中一只是小狗。|We have three pets, one of which is a puppy.
响|xiǎng|loud|餐厅里的音乐开得很响。|The music in the restaurant is very loud.
新鲜|xīn xiān|fresh|超市里的鱼很新鲜。|The fish in the supermarket is very fresh.
鸭肉|yā ròu|duck (meat)|这个店的鸭肉很好吃。|This shop's duck is delicious.
附近|fù jìn|nearby|我家附近有三个银行。|There are three banks near my home.
酒吧|jiǔ bā|pub|中学生不能去酒吧。|Secondary students can't go to pubs.
搞笑|gǎo xiào|funny; do comical acts|他经常讲故事搞笑。|He often tells stories to make people laugh.
简单|jiǎn dān|simple|我觉得科学考试很简单。|I think the science exam is easy.
*前面|qián miàn|in front|学校前面有一个车站。|There's a bus stop in front of the school.
*组屋|zǔ wū|(Singapore) public housing flats|大部分的新加坡人住在组屋。|Most Singaporeans live in HDB flats.
