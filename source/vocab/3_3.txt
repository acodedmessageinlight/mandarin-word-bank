#3.3|我的邻居|My Neighbours
听说|tīng shuō|hear of (something)|听说小花有了新朋友。|I heard Xiaohua has a new friend.
搬家|bān jiā|move house|下个周末我们搬家。|We're moving house next weekend.
邻居|lín jū|neighbour|我的邻居很喜欢小孩子。|My neighbour loves children.
谁|shuí|who|我的邻居对谁都很友好。|My neighbour is friendly to everyone.
蛋糕|dàn gāo|cake|昨天我买了一个冰淇淋蛋糕。|Yesterday I bought an ice-cream cake.
不错|bú cuò|not bad; quite good|这个花园真不错！|This garden is really nice!
哪天|nǎ tiān|some day|哪天我们一起去游泳吧！|Let's go swimming together some day!
巧克力|qiǎo kè lì|chocolate|我最爱吃巧克力。|I love chocolate most.
楼下|lóu xià|downstairs|我家楼下的邻居很不友好。|The neighbours downstairs are very unfriendly.
已经|yǐ jīng|already|我已经学了六年法文了。|I've already studied French for six years.
腿|tuǐ|leg|他的腿很长，跑步很快。|He has long legs and runs fast.
架子|jià zi|Zimmer frame; walker|奶奶用架子来帮助走路。|Grandma uses a walking frame to help her walk.
年轻|nián qīng|young|我的数学老师很年轻。|My maths teacher is very young.
白|bái|white|奶奶长着白头发。|Grandma has white hair.
脾气|pí qi|temper|我的爸爸脾气很不好。|My dad has a bad temper.
昨天|zuó tiān|yesterday|昨天我上学迟到了。|I was late for school yesterday.
捉迷藏|zhuō mí cáng|hide and seek|我妹妹喜欢玩捉迷藏。|My little sister likes playing hide and seek.
喊|hǎn|shout; yell|她一生气就大喊大叫。|As soon as she gets angry she shouts and yells.
不要|bú yào|do not|上课不要玩电脑。|Don't play on the computer in class.
吵|chǎo|noisy|邻居觉得孩子们很吵。|The neighbours think the children are noisy.
只好|zhǐ hǎo|have to; be forced to|我没带钱包，今天只好不吃饭了。|I didn't bring my wallet, so I'll have to skip lunch today.
扔球|rēng qiú|throw a ball|我和小朋友一起玩扔球。|I play catch with the little kids.
不小心|bù xiǎo xīn|inadvertently; accidentally|我不小心把他家的门弄坏了。|I accidentally broke his door.
道歉|dào qiàn|apologise|妈妈带我去邻居家道歉。|Mum took me to the neighbour's to apologise.
淘气|táo qì|naughty; mischievous|我的弟弟很淘气，也很可爱。|My little brother is naughty but cute.
碰到|pèng dào|meet (someone) by chance|昨天我碰到了我的小学老师。|Yesterday I ran into my primary school teacher.
手机|shǒu jī|mobile phone|上课不能玩手机。|You can't play on your phone in class.
认识|rèn shi|know (someone)|你认识那个白头发的爷爷吗？|Do you know that white-haired old man?
输|shū|lose|昨天的篮球比赛，我们输了。|We lost yesterday's basketball game.
不肯|bù kěn|refuse|弟弟今天不肯上学。|My little brother refused to go to school today.
保安|bǎo ān|security officer|这个保安很年轻，也很有耐心。|This security guard is young and patient.
栋|dòng|(measure word for buildings)|这栋楼很高，有250米。|This building is very tall, 250 metres.
*楼上|lóu shàng|upstairs|楼上的邻居很吵。|The neighbours upstairs are noisy.
*上楼|shàng lóu|to go upstairs|我们上楼去吧。|Let's go upstairs.
*下楼|xià lóu|to go downstairs|妈妈叫我下楼吃饭。|Mum called me downstairs to eat.
*上个|shàng ge|last (上个周末, 上个月)|上个周末我们搬家了。|We moved house last weekend.
*下个|xià ge|next (下个周末, 下个月)|下个月我去中国。|Next month I'm going to China.
