#10.1|青少年的问题和压力|Teenagers' Issues
混血儿|hùn xuè ér|a person of mixed blood|这个歌手是一个混血儿。|This singer is of mixed heritage.
身材|shēn cái|(body) figure|她总觉得自己的身材不够好。|She always feels her figure isn't good enough.
偏重|piān zhòng|overweight|他有点偏重，所以想减肥。|He's a bit overweight, so he wants to lose weight.
讨好|tǎo hǎo|try to please|他从来不愿意去讨好别人。|He's never willing to curry favour with others.
努力|nǔ lì|make great efforts|我正在努力学习中文。|I'm working hard at learning Chinese.
减肥|jiǎn féi|lose weight|姐姐正在努力减肥。|My sister is working hard to lose weight.
水煮蛋|shuǐ zhǔ dàn|boiled egg|你想吃水煮蛋还是煎蛋？|Would you like a boiled egg or a fried egg?
心爱的|xīn ài de|beloved|昨天我心爱的小狗走丢了，真叫人伤心。|Yesterday my beloved puppy got lost; it's heartbreaking.
忍|rěn|endure|这里的工作有点辛苦，你就忍着点吧。|The work here is a bit hard; just bear with it.
健身房|jiàn shēn fáng|gym|虽然我家楼下就有一个健身房，但是我从来没有去过。|Although there's a gym downstairs, I've never been.
关心|guān xīn|care about|老师很关心学生们的学习。|The teacher cares a lot about the students' learning.
演唱会|yǎn chàng huì|concert|下个星期S.H.E.要来开演唱会啦！|S.H.E. is coming to give a concert next week!
买票|mǎi piào|buy tickets|我先去买票，你在这里等我。|I'll go buy tickets first; wait for me here.
唱片|chàng piàn|song album|现在几乎没有人买唱片了。|Hardly anyone buys albums now.
又吵又闹|yòu chǎo yòu nào|make a din|他在这里又吵又闹，真的很烦人。|He's making a racket here; it's really annoying.
只有|zhǐ yǒu|only|只有他知道这件事。|Only he knows about this.
没面子|méi miàn zi|lose face|他怎么邀请，她都不去，让他觉得很没面子。|No matter how he invited her, she wouldn't go, which made him lose face.
主见|zhǔ jiàn|one's own opinion|他是一个独立、有主见的人。|He's an independent person with his own opinions.
追星|zhuī xīng|pursue a celebrity|她喜欢追星，对学业没什么兴趣。|She likes following celebrities and isn't interested in her studies.
不一定|bù yí dìng|not necessarily|太多运动不一定对身体好。|Too much exercise isn't necessarily good for you.
减轻|jiǎn qīng|reduce|听歌可以帮助我们减轻压力。|Listening to music can help us reduce stress.
现象|xiàn xiàng|phenomenon|学生做暑期工的现象很普遍。|It's common for students to do summer jobs.
棍子|gùn zi|rod; stick|他用棍子把那条蛇打死了。|He killed the snake with a stick.
