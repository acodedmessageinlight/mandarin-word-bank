#7.1|旅行的准备|Trip Preparation
公司|gōng sī|company|爸爸在这家公司工作。|Dad works at this company.
营业员|yíng yè yuán|salesperson|这个营业员的服务非常好。|This salesperson's service is excellent.
国际|guó jì|international|我在国际学校上学。|I study at an international school.
饭店|fàn diàn|hotel|这家饭店靠近地铁站，出入非常方便。|This hotel is near the metro station, very convenient.
行李|xíng li|luggage|姐姐每次旅行都带很多行李，里面都是衣服。|My sister brings lots of luggage every trip, all full of clothes.
面包车|miàn bāo chē|van|这辆面包车可以坐八到十个人。|This van can seat eight to ten people.
司机|sī jī|driver|这个司机开车非常小心。|This driver drives very carefully.
分钟|fēn zhōng|minute|电影五分钟后就要开始了。|The film starts in five minutes.
大厅|dà tīng|hall|假日酒店的大厅很大，人很多。|The Holiday Inn's lobby is big and crowded.
暑假|shǔ jià|summer holidays|去年暑假我们去了很多地方。|Last summer holiday we went to lots of places.
旅行|lǚ xíng|travel; tour|今年夏天我们要去中国旅行。|This summer we're travelling to China.
负责|fù zé|be responsible for|妈妈每天负责准备我们的晚餐。|Mum is responsible for making our dinner every day.
首先|shǒu xiān|firstly|我们将首先坐飞机，然后坐船去那个地方。|We will first fly, then take a boat there.
决定|jué dìng|decide|我决定从明天开始要好好学习。|I've decided to study hard starting tomorrow.
欧洲|Ōu Zhōu|Europe|欧洲有很多美丽的国家。|Europe has many beautiful countries.
寒假|hán jià|winter holidays|今年寒假你有什么打算？|What are your plans for this winter holiday?
订机票|dìng jī piào|book air tickets|我打算下个月去欧洲旅行，现在要上网订机票。|I plan to travel to Europe next month and need to book flights online now.
护照|hù zhào|passport|出国旅行一定要带护照。|You must bring your passport when travelling abroad.
过期|guò qī|expire|糟糕，我的护照过期了。|Oh no, my passport has expired.
上涨|shàng zhǎng|increase (in price)|今年去泰国的机票价格上涨了百分之六。|Flights to Thailand went up by six percent this year.
酒店|jiǔ diàn|hotel|这家酒店的客房很贵，我们还是找别家吧。|This hotel's rooms are expensive; let's find another.
资料|zī liào|information; data|让我先上网找些资料，然后我们再决定去哪些地方。|Let me look up some information online first, then we'll decide where to go.
地图|dì tú|map|有了这张地图，我们就不怕迷路了。|With this map, we won't worry about getting lost.
选择|xuǎn zé|choose|越多选择往往就越难选择。|The more choices there are, the harder it often is to choose.
旅馆|lǚ guǎn|inn|这家旅馆的客房很便宜，所以非常受欢迎。|This inn's rooms are cheap, so it's very popular.
信用卡|xìn yòng kǎ|credit card|我妈妈有很多张信用卡。|My mum has lots of credit cards.
付款|fù kuǎn|make payment|买东西的时候用信用卡付款比较方便。|Paying by credit card is more convenient when shopping.
原来|yuán lái|as it turns out; actually|原来出国旅行需要准备这么多东西！|So travelling abroad needs this much preparation!
忘记|wàng jì|forget|我早上忘记带钱包出门，现在没钱买东西吃。|I forgot my wallet this morning and now have no money for food.
时差|shí chā|time difference; jet lag|中国和新加坡没有时差，但和新西兰有四个小时的时差。|China and Singapore have no time difference, but there's a four-hour difference with New Zealand.
