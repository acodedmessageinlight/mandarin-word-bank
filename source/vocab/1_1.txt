#1.1|我|About Me
你|nǐ|you (singular)|你好！|Hello!
你好|nǐ hǎo|hello|你好！我是多多。|Hello! I'm Duoduo.
我|wǒ|I; me|我是凯瑞。|I'm Kerry.
是|shì|am; is; are (verb "to be")|我是中国人。|I'm Chinese.
叫|jiào|call; be called|我叫多多。|My name is Duoduo.
人|rén|person, people|我是英国人。|I'm British.
住|zhù|live|我住在英国。|I live in the UK.
在|zài|at; in|我住在中国。|I live in China.
哪里|nǎ lǐ|where|你住在哪里？|Where do you live?
生日|shēng rì|birthday|我的生日是三月二十五号。|My birthday is 25 March.
月|yuè|month; moon|四月六日是我的生日。|6 April is my birthday.
号|hào|date (spoken Chinese)|十二月二十号是多多的生日。|20 December is Duoduo's birthday.
几|jǐ|how many|你的生日是几月几号？|When is your birthday?
日|rì|date (written Chinese)|我的生日是九月五日。|My birthday is 5 September.
多大|duō dà|how old|你多大？|How old are you?
岁|suì|year (of age)|我九岁。|I'm nine years old.
今年|jīn nián|this year|我今年十二岁。|I'm twelve this year.
年级|nián jí|grade; level|我上十年级。|I'm in Year 10.
上学|shàng xué|attend school|我不喜欢上学。|I don't like going to school.
喜欢|xǐ huan|like|我不喜欢我的哥哥。|I don't like my older brother.
我的|wǒ de|my|我的学校很大。|My school is big.
学校|xué xiào|school|我喜欢我的学校。|I like my school.
学|xué|learn, study|我喜欢学中文。|I like learning Chinese.
会|huì|can; be able to|你会说中文吗？|Can you speak Chinese?
说|shuō|speak|我们不说英语，好吗？|Let's not speak English, OK?
不|bù|no; not|我不会写字。|I can't write characters.
大家|dà jiā|all; everyone|我们大家都喜欢学中文。|We all like learning Chinese.
介绍|jiè shào|introduce|你能介绍一下你的好朋友吗？|Can you introduce your best friend?
自己|zì jǐ|oneself|我介绍一下我自己。|Let me introduce myself.
姓|xìng|surname|你姓什么？|What's your surname?
来自|lái zì|come from|我来自中国。|I come from China.
出生|chū shēng|be born|你是在哪一年出生的？|Which year were you born?
去过|qù guo|have been to|我去过日本，没有去过韩国。|I've been to Japan but not to Korea.
等|děng|and so on|我去过中国、美国、英国等国家。|I've been to China, the US, the UK and other countries.
和|hé|and|昨天我和小丽去学校。|Yesterday Xiaoli and I went to school.
最|zuì|the most|你最喜欢吃什么？|What do you like eating most?

*短|duǎn|short|哥哥的头发很短。|My brother's hair is short.
