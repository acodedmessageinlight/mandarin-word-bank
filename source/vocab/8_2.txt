#8.2|城市的不同地方|Different Places in a City
魅力|mèi lì|charm|香港是一个很有魅力的城市。|Hong Kong is a very charming city.
中等|zhōng děng|medium|这是个中等城市，没有上海大。|This is a medium-sized city, not as big as Shanghai.
步行街|bù xíng jiē|pedestrian zone|我喜欢去步行街逛逛。|I like strolling along the pedestrian street.
商店|shāng diàn|shop|这里的商店很多，可以逛一整天。|There are lots of shops here; you could browse all day.
应有尽有|yīng yǒu jìn yǒu|have everything that one could wish for|这个集市的东西应有尽有。|This market has everything you could want.
东边|dōng biān|east|太阳从东边升起。|The sun rises in the east.
拍照|pāi zhào|take photos|同学们都喜欢拍照，我不喜欢。|My classmates all like taking photos; I don't.
留念|liú niàn|keep as a memento|我拍了很多照片作为留念。|I took lots of photos as souvenirs.
地标|dì biāo|landmark|台北101是台北的地标。|Taipei 101 is Taipei's landmark.
顶楼|dǐng lóu|top floor|要上顶楼才能看到整个城市的夜景。|You have to go to the top floor to see the whole city's night view.
夜景|yè jǐng|night view|香港山顶的夜景令人难忘。|The night view from Hong Kong's Peak is unforgettable.
美不胜收|měi bú shèng shōu|more beauty than one can take in|从山顶望去，这个城市的景色美不胜收。|Seen from the mountaintop, the city's scenery is breathtaking.
博物馆|bó wù guǎn|museum|我每到一个地方，都喜欢去当地的博物馆。|Wherever I go, I like visiting the local museum.
科技|kē jì|technology|现代科技让我们的生活更加便利。|Modern technology makes our lives more convenient.
自然|zì rán|nature; natural|大自然美不胜收。|Nature is breathtakingly beautiful.
放松|fàng sōng|relax|最近压力很大，要好好放松一下。|I've been under a lot of pressure lately; I need to relax.
增长|zēng zhǎng|increase; grow|这个城市今年经济增长很快。|This city's economy grew fast this year.
知识|zhī shi|knowledge|学习新知识是一件很开心的事情。|Learning new things is a joy.
*南京路|Nán jīng Lù|Nanjing Road (famous shopping street in Shanghai)|南京路是上海有名的步行街。|Nanjing Road is Shanghai's famous pedestrian street.
*外滩|Wài Tān|the Bund|很多人喜欢在外滩拍照留念。|Many people like taking photos at the Bund.
*浦东|Pǔ Dōng|Pudong (business district in Shanghai)|浦东有很多高楼大厦。|Pudong has lots of skyscrapers.
*东方明珠塔|Dōng fāng Míng zhū Tǎ|Oriental Pearl TV Tower|东方明珠塔是上海的地标之一。|The Oriental Pearl Tower is one of Shanghai's landmarks.
到处|dào chù|everywhere|星期天，购物中心里到处都是人。|On Sunday the shopping centre is packed everywhere.
迷路|mí lù|lose one's way|昨天我上街的时候迷路了，很晚才回家。|I got lost in town yesterday and got home very late.
方法|fāng fǎ|way; method|要学好中文有很多不同的方法。|There are many different ways to learn Chinese well.
问路|wèn lù|ask for directions|出门旅游时我经常问路。|I often ask for directions when travelling.
清楚|qīng chu|clear|他的话说得不清楚，我不知道他要什么。|He didn't speak clearly; I don't know what he wants.
判断|pàn duàn|judge; decide|很难判断她是不是真的喜欢打篮球。|It's hard to tell whether she really likes basketball.
方向|fāng xiàng|direction|请问地铁站是在哪个方向？|Excuse me, which direction is the metro station?
自信|zì xìn|confidence|我的妹妹很自信，学习也很努力。|My sister is very confident and studies hard.
往前走|wǎng qián zǒu|go straight|你只要往前走五分钟，就会看到地铁站了。|Just walk straight for five minutes and you'll see the metro station.
路口|lù kǒu|intersection; crossing|到了这个路口，我迷路了。|When I reached this intersection, I got lost.
往右拐|wǎng yòu guǎi|turn right|去医院该往右拐，还是往左拐呢？|To get to the hospital, should I turn right or left?
一直走|yì zhí zǒu|keep walking|当地人告诉我，一直走，再走两分钟就到了。|A local told me to keep walking; two more minutes and I'd be there.
继续|jì xù|continue|他虽然生病了，但还是继续工作。|Although he was ill, he kept working.
左|zuǒ|left|请在前面的路口左拐。|Please turn left at the next intersection.
转|zhuǎn|turn|从这里往右转，就能看到地铁站。|Turn right here and you'll see the metro station.
相对的|xiāng duì de|relatively|好和坏是相对的，有时坏事会变好事。|Good and bad are relative; sometimes a bad thing turns into a good one.
普通话|pǔ tōng huà|Putonghua; Mandarin; standard Chinese|你听得懂普通话吗？|Can you understand Mandarin?
听不懂|tīng bù dǒng|cannot understand (by listening)|他听不懂普通话，我们还是用英语吧。|He doesn't understand Mandarin; let's use English.
理解|lǐ jiě|understand|我觉得爸爸不理解我。他不知道我需要什么。|I feel Dad doesn't understand me. He doesn't know what I need.
不过|bú guò|but; however|我的老师很严格，不过他也很关心学生。|My teacher is strict, but he also cares about his students.
逛|guàng|stroll; roam|我妈妈喜欢逛街，一逛就是好几个小时。|My mum loves going shopping; once she starts, it's hours.
