#9.2|做义工和暑期工|Volunteering and Summer Jobs
义工|yì gōng|volunteer work|做义工可以让你很开心。|Volunteering can make you very happy.
养老院|yǎng lǎo yuàn|old people's home|这个城市有几家养老院？|How many old people's homes are there in this city?
看望|kàn wàng|visit (someone)|明天我要去医院看望生病的阿姨。|Tomorrow I'm visiting my sick aunt in hospital.
老人|lǎo rén|old people; seniors|养老院里住着很多老人。|Many elderly people live in the old people's home.
孤儿院|gū ér yuàn|orphanage|我在想我们能为孤儿院里的孤儿做什么？|I'm thinking about what we can do for the orphans in the orphanage.
照顾|zhào gù|look after|这个暑假我想好好照顾生病的奶奶。|This summer I want to take good care of my sick grandma.
医院|yī yuàn|hospital|这个医院有很多有名的医生。|This hospital has many famous doctors.
病人|bìng rén|patient|这些病人都需要护士的照顾。|These patients all need nurses' care.
几乎|jī hū|almost|他几乎每个周末都到老人院看望老人。|He visits the elderly at the old people's home almost every weekend.
第一次|dì yī cì|first time|这是我第一次到孤儿院做义工。|This is my first time volunteering at an orphanage.
热情|rè qíng|enthusiastic|这些义工都非常热情。|These volunteers are all very enthusiastic.
说话|shuō huà|talk|他很内向，不喜欢和人说话。|He's introverted and doesn't like talking to people.
困难|kùn nan|difficulty|没有什么困难能难倒我。|No difficulty can defeat me.
微笑|wēi xiào|smile|她的脸上常常带着微笑。|She often has a smile on her face.
一会儿|yí huìr|brief moment|你在这里等我，我一会儿就回来。|Wait for me here; I'll be back in a moment.
希望|xī wàng|hope|我希望早点考试，然后我就可以出国旅行了。|I hope the exams come soon, then I can travel abroad.
暑期工|shǔ qī gōng|summer job|他做的暑期工是在餐厅当服务员。|His summer job is working as a waiter in a restaurant.
意见|yì jiàn|opinion; view|你对做暑期工有什么意见？|What's your opinion on doing a summer job?
想法|xiǎng fǎ|opinion; what one has in mind|我不同意你的想法。|I don't agree with your idea.
极其|jí qí|extremely|现在要找到一份好的工作极其困难。|It's extremely hard to find a good job now.
兼职|jiān zhí|part-time|我根本没有时间去做兼职。|I simply have no time for a part-time job.
经验|jīng yàn|experience|有工作经验非常重要。|Having work experience is very important.
挑剔|tiāo tì|picky|他怎么还是这么挑剔？|Why is he still so picky?
愿意|yuàn yì|willing|他不愿意做这么辛苦的工作。|He isn't willing to do such hard work.
绝对|jué duì|absolutely|多看书对我们绝对有好处。|Reading more is absolutely good for us.
锻炼|duàn liàn|train; exercise|只有天天锻炼，身体才会好。|Only by exercising every day will you be healthy.
扩大|kuò dà|expand|大量阅读能扩大知识面。|Reading widely can broaden your knowledge.
交际圈|jiāo jì quān|social circle|不要一直呆在家里了，出去走走才能扩大交际圈。|Don't stay at home all the time; go out to widen your social circle.
只要|zhǐ yào|so long as|只要明天不下雨，我们就去外面打篮球。|As long as it doesn't rain tomorrow, we'll play basketball outside.
社会经验|shè huì jīng yàn|social experience|她刚工作，还没有什么社会经验。|She's just started working and doesn't have much life experience yet.
骗|piàn|deceive; con|她对你这么好，你怎么可以骗她呢？|She's so good to you; how can you deceive her?
辛辛苦苦|xīn xīn kǔ kǔ|laboriously|这可是我辛辛苦苦做的菜，再难吃也试一下吧？|I worked hard on this dish; even if it's bad, at least try it?
工资|gōng zī|wages|在餐馆打工的工资不是很高。|Wages for working in a restaurant aren't very high.
服务员|fú wù yuán|waiter/waitress|这家餐馆的服务员都很年轻。|The waiters at this restaurant are all young.
客服|kè fú|customer service|这间店的客服不太好，我们还是不要买它的东西吧。|This shop's customer service is poor; let's not buy from it.
技能|jì néng|skill|你从这份暑期工学到了什么技能？|What skills did you learn from this summer job?
浪费|làng fèi|waste|手机聊天真浪费时间。|Chatting on the phone really wastes time.
掌上明珠|zhǎng shàng míng zhū|beloved daughter|她没有兄弟姐妹，是父母的掌上明珠。|She has no siblings and is the apple of her parents' eye.
娇气|jiāo qì|delicate|她是个娇气的女孩，从来没有做过家务。|She's a pampered girl who has never done housework.
独立|dú lì|independent|她非常独立，常常一个人去旅行。|She's very independent and often travels alone.
睡懒觉|shuì lǎn jiào|have a good sleep|周末能够睡懒觉，真幸福啊。|Being able to sleep in at the weekend is bliss.
聚会|jù huì|get-together; party|下星期有个同学聚会，可以看到以前中学的同学。|There's a class reunion next week; I can see my old secondary school classmates.
