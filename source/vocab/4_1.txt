#4.1|性格与长相|Personalities and Physical Appearances
吵架|chǎo jià|quarrel|我和好朋友从来不吵架。|My best friend and I never argue.
伤心|shāng xīn|sad|离开家让我很伤心。|Leaving home made me very sad.
慢性子|màn xìng zi|slowcoach; unexcitable|我姐姐不是慢性子。|My sister isn't a slowcoach.
急性子|jí xìng zi|impetuous person|我弟弟是急性子。|My little brother is impatient.
一直|yì zhí|continuously|我今天一直在上课。|I've been in class all day today.
催|cuī|urge; press|妈妈每天催我练钢琴。|Mum nags me to practise piano every day.
来不及|lái bù jí|not enough time (to do something)|早上我常常来不及吃早饭。|In the morning I often don't have time for breakfast.
好心|hǎo xīn|good-natured|我的奶奶很好心。|My grandma is very kind-hearted.
啰嗦|luō suō|longwinded|我不喜欢啰嗦的人。|I don't like people who go on and on.
停|tíng|stop|雨一直下个不停。|The rain won't stop.
外向|wài xiàng|extroverted|我哥哥很外向。|My brother is very outgoing.
内向|nèi xiàng|introverted|我有一点儿内向。|I'm a bit introverted.
难讲|nán jiǎng|hard to say|他是不是好人，很难讲。|It's hard to say whether he's a good person.
偶像|ǒu xiàng|idol|现在的年轻人都有自己的偶像。|Young people today all have their own idols.
年代|nián dài|era|这个年代的年轻人都有手机。|Young people of this era all have phones.
跳舞|tiào wǔ|dancing|我的爱好是跳舞。|My hobby is dancing.
崇拜|chóng bài|adore; idolise|你崇拜的偶像是谁？|Who is the idol you admire?
大多数|dà duō shù|majority|大多数人都喜欢音乐。|Most people like music.
酷|kù|cool|他穿得很酷，说话也很酷。|He dresses cool and talks cool.
另类|lìng lèi|unusual; unconventional|他是一个很另类的人。|He's a very unconventional person.
歌手|gē shǒu|singer|这个歌手有很多歌迷。|This singer has lots of fans.
不约而同|bù yuē ér tóng|coincidentally|我和好朋友不约而同地穿一样的衣服。|My best friend and I happened to wear the same clothes.
离开|lí kāi|leave|我的好朋友要离开这个学校了。|My best friend is going to leave this school.
故意|gù yì|on purpose; intentionally|他故意说不喜欢她。|He deliberately said he didn't like her.
同情|tóng qíng|sympathise|他很同情那个伤心的人。|He sympathises with that sad person.
帅哥|shuài gē|good-looking man|这几个帅哥学习好，运动也好。|These handsome guys are good at study and sport.
乐队|yuè duì|musical band|你最喜欢哪个乐队？|Which band do you like most?
迷人|mí rén|charming; mesmerising|大家都说我的眼睛很迷人。|Everyone says my eyes are charming.
不合群|bù hé qún|anti-social|新来的那个同学很不合群。|That new classmate is very unsociable.
性格|xìng gé|personality|他的性格很好，很合群。|He has a good personality and gets on well with people.
调皮|tiáo pí|mischievous|我的小狗很调皮，也很可爱。|My puppy is mischievous but cute.
刺青|cì qīng|tattoo|有人觉得刺青很酷。|Some people think tattoos are cool.
难过|nán guò|sad|我考试没考好，觉得很难过。|I did badly in the exam and feel sad.
洗手间|xǐ shǒu jiān|toilet; washroom|洗手间也可叫作卫生间。|A washroom can also be called 卫生间.
