#2.3|课外活动|Extra-Curricular Activities
课外活动|kè wài huó dòng|extra-curricular activity|你喜欢什么课外活动？|What extracurricular activities do you like?
羽毛球|yǔ máo qiú|badminton|我喜欢打羽毛球。|I like playing badminton.
打|dǎ|play|同学们都喜欢打球。|My classmates all like playing ball games.
星期五|xīng qī wǔ|Friday|星期五我们有足球比赛。|We have a football match on Friday.
参加|cān jiā|join; take part|你参加了什么课外活动？|What extracurricular activities did you join?
长跑|cháng pǎo|long distance running|他觉得长跑很难。|He thinks long-distance running is hard.
板球|bǎn qiú|cricket|我打板球打得很好。|I play cricket very well.
明年|míng nián|next year|我明年去英国上学。|Next year I'm going to school in the UK.
中学会考|zhōng xué huì kǎo|general examination for secondary schools|马上要中学会考了。|The secondary school exams are coming up soon.
忙|máng|busy|你爸爸忙吗？|Is your dad busy?
应该|yīng gāi|should; ought to|你应该要好好学习。|You should study hard.
准备|zhǔn bèi|prepare|周末我在家准备考试。|At the weekend I prepare for exams at home.
特别|tè bié|exceptionally|他的科学特别好。|He's exceptionally good at science.
帮助|bāng zhù|help|我姐姐常常帮助我。|My older sister often helps me.
怎么|zěn me|how|你怎么了？|What's wrong with you?
不可开交|bù kě kāi jiāo|be locked in; be tied up (used after 得 as its complement)|你怎么总是忙得不可开交？|Why are you always so terribly busy?
放学|fàng xué|school ends|放学以后我要参加很多课外活动。|After school I take part in lots of activities.
以后|yǐ hòu|after|我下课以后去打球。|After class I go and play ball.
训练|xùn liàn|training|我每天都有网球训练。|I have tennis training every day.
每次|měi cì|every time|我每次考试都考得很好。|I do well in every exam.
小时|xiǎo shí|hour|星期三我有篮球训练，每次两个小时。|I have basketball training on Wednesdays, two hours each time.
千米|qiān mǐ|1000 metres|今天我跑了一千米。|I ran a kilometre today.
弹钢琴|tán gāng qín|play the piano|我姐姐很喜欢弹钢琴。|My older sister loves playing the piano.
合唱团|hé chàng tuán|choir|我在合唱团里唱歌。|I sing in the choir.
排练|pái liàn|rehearsal|明天学校有排练。|There's a rehearsal at school tomorrow.
周末|zhōu mò|weekend|周末我要准备考试。|I need to prepare for exams at the weekend.
比赛|bǐ sài|competition|星期五有排球比赛。|There's a volleyball match on Friday.
队长|duì zhǎng|captain|我们学校篮球队的队长很帅。|Our school basketball captain is handsome.
*上午|shàng wǔ|morning|星期六上午我有比赛。|I have a match on Saturday morning.
*中午|zhōng wǔ|noon|中午我有乒乓球活动。|At noon I have table tennis.
*下午|xià wǔ|afternoon|星期天下午我有比赛。|I have a match on Sunday afternoon.
*晚上|wǎn shang|evening; night|星期五晚上我有排球训练。|I have volleyball training on Friday evening.
*网球|wǎng qiú|tennis|我喜欢打网球。|I like playing tennis.
*排球|pái qiú|volleyball|我是排球队的队长。|I'm the volleyball team captain.
*乒乓球|pīng pāng qiú|table tennis|中午我们打乒乓球。|We play table tennis at noon.
*橄榄球|gǎn lǎn qiú|rugby|星期四我有橄榄球训练。|I have rugby training on Thursday.
*足球|zú qiú|football|我们去踢足球吧。|Let's go and play football.
*只有|zhǐ yǒu|only have|我只有一个哥哥。|I only have one older brother.
*只是|zhǐ shì|just|我只是看看，不买。|I'm just looking, not buying.
*只好|zhǐ hǎo|have to|下大雨，我只好不出门。|It's pouring, so I have to stay in.
