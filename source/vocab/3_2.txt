#3.2|交通工具|Modes of Transport
得|děi|must; have to|我得参加比赛。|I have to take part in the competition.|嘚
快|kuài|fast|坐车比走路快。|Going by car is faster than walking.
坐|zuò|go by; travel by|我天天坐出租车上学。|I take a taxi to school every day.
出租车|chū zū chē|taxi|路上有很多出租车。|There are lots of taxis on the road.
公共汽车|gōng gòng qì chē|public bus|公共汽车可以坐很多人。|A bus can carry lots of people.
便宜|pián yi|cheap|他的文具盒很便宜，但是很漂亮。|His pencil case is cheap but pretty.
慢|màn|slow|我妈妈游泳游得很慢。|My mum swims slowly.
地铁|dì tiě|underground; tube; subway|地铁又快又便宜。|The metro is fast and cheap.
走路|zǒu lù|walk|老师天天走路上班。|The teacher walks to work every day.
地铁站|dì tiě zhàn|underground station|地铁站很远，我不想走过去。|The metro station is far; I don't want to walk there.
远|yuǎn|far|我家离学校很远。|My home is far from school.
骑|qí|ride|我喜欢骑马。|I like riding horses.
自行车|zì xíng chē|bicycle|自行车比汽车慢，但是比走路快。|A bike is slower than a car, but faster than walking.
电车|diàn chē|tram|现在电车很少见了。|Trams are rare now.
的士|dí shì|taxi|的士就是出租车。|的士 means taxi.
城市|chéng shì|city|城市中有各种各样的车。|There are all kinds of vehicles in the city.
交通工具|jiāo tōng gōng jù|mode of transport|我最常用的交通工具是地铁。|The transport I use most is the metro.
比如|bǐ rú|for example; such as|我学很多科目，比如英文、中文、数学等等。|I study many subjects, such as English, Chinese and maths.
渡轮|dù lún|ferry|昨天我们坐渡轮去了一个小岛。|Yesterday we took a ferry to an island.
等等|děng děng|and so on; etc.|课外活动有游泳、篮球、足球等等。|Activities include swimming, basketball, football and so on.
上班|shàng bān|go to work|我每天骑车上班。|I cycle to work every day.
如果|rú guǒ|if|如果不用上补习班，该多好！|How nice it would be if I didn't have to go to tutoring!
可以|kě yǐ|can|学校不远，我可以走路上学。|School isn't far; I can walk there.
时间|shí jiān|time|时间不多了，坐出租车去吧！|We're short of time; let's take a taxi!
虽然|suī rán|although|虽然生病，我还是去考试了。|Although I was ill, I still went to the exam.
塞车|sāi chē|traffic jam|塞车的时候，我在路上等了三个小时。|In the traffic jam I waited on the road for three hours.
小岛|xiǎo dǎo|island|放假的时候我想去小岛玩。|In the holidays I want to visit an island.
或者|huò zhě|or|我妈妈开车或者坐地铁上班。|My mum drives or takes the metro to work.
跑步|pǎo bù|run; jog|每天早上我跑步半小时。|I run for half an hour every morning.
离|lí|(in describing distances) from|我家离地铁站很远。|My home is far from the metro station.
近|jìn|near|游泳池离网球场很近。|The pool is close to the tennis court.
以前|yǐ qián|previously; formerly|以前我骑车上学。|I used to cycle to school.
王国|wáng guó|kingdom|中国现在正成为汽车的王国。|China is now becoming a kingdom of cars.
健身|jiàn shēn|exercise; body building|我每天都去健身一个小时。|I work out for an hour every day.
环保|huán bǎo|environmental protection|我们大家都要环保。|We should all protect the environment.
有钱|yǒu qián|rich|这个城市有钱的人越来越多。|There are more and more rich people in this city.
结果|jié guǒ|in the end; as a result|我起床晚了，结果上学迟到了。|I got up late, so I was late for school.
为了|wèi le|in order to|为了会考，我每天都做作业到很晚。|For the exams, I do homework until late every day.
拥挤|yōng jǐ|crowded|放学后，学校的小卖部里很拥挤。|After school the tuck shop is very crowded.
买得起|mǎi de qǐ|able to afford|有钱就能买得起那台电脑。|With money you can afford that computer.
宽|kuān|broad|这条路有十米宽。|This road is ten metres wide.
免费|miǎn fèi|free of charge|天下没有免费的午餐。|There's no such thing as a free lunch.
一举两得|yì jǔ liǎng dé|kill two birds with one stone|骑车上班既能健身，又能省钱，真是一举两得。|Cycling to work keeps you fit and saves money; it kills two birds with one stone.
