#5.2|外出就餐|Dining Out
那些|nà xiē|those|那些人不喜欢吃中餐。|Those people don't like Chinese food.
所有的|suǒ yǒu de|all|所有的人都说学校餐厅的西餐好吃。|Everyone says the Western food in the canteen is good.
菜|cài|dish|海鲜饭是这家餐馆的特色菜。|Seafood rice is this restaurant's speciality dish.
味道|wèi dào|taste|这家餐馆的包子味道很好。|The buns at this restaurant taste great.
把|bǎ|(used to move the object before the verb)|我把作业做完了。|I've finished my homework.
改变|gǎi biàn|change|我的弟弟这一年改变了很多。|My brother has changed a lot this year.
炒饭|chǎo fàn|fried rice|做炒饭很简单。|Making fried rice is easy.
炒面|chǎo miàn|fried noodles|我今天的晚餐是炒面。|My dinner today is fried noodles.
舅舅|jiù jiu|uncle (mother's brother)|我的舅舅是老师，不是厨师。|My uncle is a teacher, not a chef.
厨师|chú shī|chef|那个厨师做的菜特别好吃。|That chef's dishes are especially tasty.
四川菜|Sì chuān cài|Sichuan cuisine|四川菜非常有特色。|Sichuan food is very distinctive.
饺子|jiǎo zi|dumpling|这个餐馆的饺子美味可口。|This restaurant's dumplings are delicious.
北方|běi fāng|northern part|北京在中国的北方。|Beijing is in northern China.
常见|cháng jiàn|common|油条豆浆是中国常见的早餐。|Dough sticks and soy milk are a common breakfast in China.
做法|zuò fǎ|method|炒面的做法很简单。|Fried noodles are easy to make.
需要|xū yào|need; require|做牛排需要用很好的牛肉。|To make steak you need good beef.
材料|cái liào|ingredient|你有做蛋糕的材料吗？|Do you have the ingredients for a cake?
调料|tiáo liào|seasoning|我觉得放太多调料不健康。|I think too much seasoning is unhealthy.
如下|rú xià|as follows|海鲜饭的做法如下。|The method for seafood rice is as follows.
切碎|qiē suì|mince|请把牛肉和洋葱都切碎。|Please chop the beef and onion finely.
馅|xiàn|filling|你喜欢吃什么馅的饺子？|What filling do you like in dumplings?
烧开|shāo kāi|boil|先把水烧开，再下面条。|First boil the water, then add the noodles.
冷水|lěng shuǐ|cold water|不要总是喝冷水。|Don't always drink cold water.
再次|zài cì|again; one more time|我昨天再次去吃中餐。|Yesterday I went for Chinese food again.
艺术|yì shù|art|电影是一门艺术。|Film is an art.
地标|dì biāo|landmark|我们城市的地标是什么？|What's our city's landmark?
咖啡|kā fēi|coffee|咖啡和茶都是人们喜欢的饮料。|Coffee and tea are both popular drinks.
阳台|yáng tái|balcony|妈妈在阳台上养了很多花。|Mum grows lots of flowers on the balcony.
黄色|huáng sè|yellow|花园里开满了黄色的花。|The garden is full of yellow flowers.
灯光|dēng guāng|lighting; illumination|书桌上的灯光很亮。|The light on the desk is bright.
优美|yōu měi|beautiful|山顶的风景很优美。|The view from the mountaintop is beautiful.
旧时代|jiù shí dài|old era|旧时代的文化有好有坏。|The culture of old times had good and bad sides.
游客|yóu kè|visitor; tourist|很多游客喜欢来这个城市。|Many tourists like coming to this city.
曾经|céng jīng|once; used to|我曾经特别爱吃四川菜。|I used to love Sichuan food.
套餐|tào cān|set meal|点套餐比较方便。|Ordering a set meal is more convenient.
欧元|ōu yuán|euro (currency)|欧元主要在欧洲的一些国家使用。|The euro is used mainly in some European countries.
营业时间|yíng yè shí jiān|business hours|这家中餐馆的营业时间很短。|This Chinese restaurant's opening hours are short.
凌晨|líng chén|the wee hours|哥哥今天凌晨还在做作业。|My brother was still doing homework in the early hours.
地址|dì zhǐ|address|你家的地址是什么？|What's your home address?
电话|diàn huà|telephone|有事就给我打电话。|Call me if anything comes up.
网址|wǎng zhǐ|web address|这个网址很有用。|This website is very useful.
类别|lèi bié|category|这本书是什么类别的？|What category is this book?
人气指数|rén qì zhǐ shù|popularity|这家餐馆的人气指数很高。|This restaurant is very popular.
*咕噜肉|gū lū ròu|sweet and sour pork|我很喜欢吃咕噜肉。|I love sweet and sour pork.
*柠檬鸡|níng méng jī|lemon chicken|柠檬鸡不是真正的中餐。|Lemon chicken isn't real Chinese food.
*菜肉饺子|cài ròu jiǎo zi|dumplings stuffed with vegetable and meat|妈妈做了菜肉饺子。|Mum made meat and vegetable dumplings.
*三鲜饺子|sān xiān jiǎo zi|dumplings stuffed with three types of fillings|三鲜饺子很好吃。|Three-delicacy dumplings are delicious.
*韭菜饺子|jiǔ cài jiǎo zi|dumplings stuffed with chives|我不喜欢韭菜饺子。|I don't like chive dumplings.
*猪肉饺子|zhū ròu jiǎo zi|dumplings stuffed with pork|北方的猪肉饺子最常见。|Pork dumplings are most common in the north.
*猪肉|zhū ròu|pork|把猪肉和韭菜切碎。|Chop the pork and chives finely.
*面粉|miàn fěn|flour|饺子皮是用面粉做的。|Dumpling wrappers are made from flour.
*饺子皮|jiǎo zi pí|dumpling skin|把馅包进饺子皮里。|Wrap the filling in the dumpling skin.
*醋|cù|vinegar|吃饺子要放醋。|Have vinegar with dumplings.
*酱油|jiàng yóu|soya sauce|在馅里加一点酱油。|Add a little soy sauce to the filling.
*盐|yán|salt|不要放太多盐。|Don't add too much salt.
*香油|xiāng yóu|sesame oil|加一点香油会更香。|A little sesame oil makes it more fragrant.
