奖励|jiǎng lì|reward|老师的鼓励是最好的奖励。|The teacher's encouragement is the best reward.
碗|wǎn|bowl|我晚餐要吃两碗饭。|I want two bowls of rice for dinner.

零食|líng shí|snack; titbits|我妹妹零食不离手，所以很胖。|My sister always has snacks in hand, so she's chubby.

有意义|yǒu yì yì|meaningful|我要把钱花在有意义的事情上。|I want to spend money on meaningful things.

儿童|ér tóng|children|你家楼下有儿童游乐场吗？|Is there a children's playground downstairs from your home?
捐款|juān kuǎn|donate|她每个月都捐款帮助那些孤独的老人。|She donates every month to help lonely elderly people.
孤独|gū dú|lonely; single or childless|他一个人过生日，非常孤独。|He spent his birthday alone and felt very lonely.
利弊|lì bì|pros and cons|什么事情都有利弊。|Everything has its pros and cons.
网购|wǎng gòu|online shopping|现在网购非常流行。|Online shopping is very popular now.

商品|shāng pǐn|products; merchandise|这家店很大，里面的商品很多。|This shop is big and has lots of products.

安全|ān quán|safety|外出旅游，一定要注意安全。|When travelling, always pay attention to safety.
缺点|quē diǎn|shortcoming|她什么都好，就是有不爱运动这个缺点。|She's good at everything; her only flaw is not liking exercise.
照片|zhào piàn|photo|这些照片是我去年到中国旅行时拍的。|I took these photos on my trip to China last year.

优点|yōu diǎn|strong point; merit|她的缺点很多，优点更多。|She has many faults, but even more strengths.
